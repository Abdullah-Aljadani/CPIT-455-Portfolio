Applying the Software Product Line (SPL) strategy to the Najm application has demonstrated that effective reuse is a balance between speed and extreme caution. While reusing core components significantly accelerates deployment and ensures consistency across insurance providers, the "Ariane 5" lesson serves as a critical reminder that integration is the most vulnerable point in software engineering. I have learned that resilience is not merely about writing new code, but about rigorously validating reused modules and enforcing strict boundary checks to prevent silent failures. Ultimately, aligning our reuse strategy with modern Micro-services architecture ensures that Najm can remain a dependable and isolated system, bridging the gap between technical efficiency and professional reliability.
