The shift from reliability to resilience in the Najm app acknowledges that while faults—like memory leaks or human fatigue—are inevitable, system failure is not. By applying the Swiss Cheese Model, we demonstrate that a resilient system doesn't rely on a single 'perfect' layer, but on the strategic misalignment of vulnerabilities across technical and human domains. This exercise proves that true service continuity is achieved through Graceful Degradation and Human-in-the-loop intervention, ensuring that even when automated systems reach their limits, the critical mission of supporting citizens during accidents remains uninterrupted.
