Transitioning to a Component-Based Software Engineering (CBSE) mindset for the Najm application has shifted my perspective from being a 'coder' to becoming a 'system architect.' I have realized that the true efficiency of a complex system like Najm lies not in writing every line of code from scratch, but in the strategic orchestration of specialized, high-quality components. Designing the 'Requires/Provides' interfaces forced me to think deeply about system boundaries and the necessity of the Adapter Pattern to mitigate the risks of external API changes. This assignment has taught me that professional wisdom in software engineering is about leveraging existing excellence to build a resilient, scalable, and cost-effective ecosystem, ensuring that the final product is greater than the sum of its parts.
