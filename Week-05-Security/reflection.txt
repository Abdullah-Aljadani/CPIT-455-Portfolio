The development of a Security Assurance Case for the Najm application highlights that robust cybersecurity is not merely a technical checkbox, but a continuous, evidence-based argument that aligns system architecture with national safety standards. By systematically analyzing potential threats like insurance fraud and data breaches, and addressing them through a defense-in-depth strategy—integrating high-level authentication like Nafath with rigorous NCA compliance—we create a framework where security is both proactive and verifiable. This process reflects the critical balance between maintaining high user accessibility during emergencies and ensuring the absolute integrity of sensitive national data, proving that public trust in digital governance is sustained only through documented, rigorous validation and constant adaptation to emerging threats.
